Where we are today

This is a proposal seeking community support. This website introduces the idea; it does not announce an operating garden or public opening.

01 / The Vision

A place to cultivate curiosity.

The vision is a public botanical garden where the appreciation of plants brings people together—a place for observation, practical learning, and the pleasure of growing things.

Its character should grow from the community it serves. Local knowledge, thoughtful partnerships, and shared effort can help turn an idea into a lasting resource for Chesapeake.

02 / Community & Education

Learning, rooted in experience.

A garden can be more than a collection of plants. It can be a place where people learn by doing, exchange skills, and discover new interests.

These are possibilities to explore with future participants and partners:

Plants & practice

Hands-on learning in plant care, propagation, and the patient work of helping living things thrive.

Art & observation

Connections among horticulture, bonsai, floral design, and the arts—different ways of seeing the natural world.

Shared knowledge

Study groups, demonstrations, and opportunities for experienced growers and curious newcomers to learn together.

Community connections

Conversations with educators, garden clubs, artists, and neighbors about what a garden could make possible.
